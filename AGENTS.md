# AGENTS.md: Rules for AI collaborators

**AGENTS v0.1, 2026-11-10**

This file is for any AI working in this repository: Claudia (Claude, in chat or Claude Code), Mara (ChatGPT or Codex), or anyone else.  Read it fully before changing anything.

## Who is who

- **Nathan (TheTexasJack):** owner and lead designer.  Makes every decision.  Alpha2-Roger is his passion project: the game he wants to play.
- **Claudia:** Claude.  Design docs, world data, tools, and game code.
- **Mara:** Nathan's ChatGPT.  Art cleanup, image work, and brainstorming.

## Start of every session

1. Pull `main`.
2. Read `STATUS.md` (where things stand, what is next).
3. Read the docs your task touches (see the map below).
4. Check `VERSIONS.md` for current versions.

## End of every session

1. Bump the version line at the top of every file you changed, and update its row in `VERSIONS.md`.
2. Update `STATUS.md` if the state or the next steps changed.
3. Commit straight to `main` with a clear message and push.

## Lanes

- **One AI per file at a time.**  If another collaborator is working on a file, leave it alone.
- Mara works in `docs/art/`.  If Mara needs a design change, she proposes it in chat or as a note; she does not edit `docs/design/` directly unless Nathan asks.
- Claudia works in `docs/design/`, `docs/plan/`, `tools/`, and `game/`.
- Nathan can change anything.

## Decisions and drafts

- A line with no marker is Nathan's decision.  Write it in his words; do not read in claims he did not make.
- A suggestion is marked **Draft (Claudia)** or **Draft (Mara)** until Nathan approves it.  Never present a draft as decided.
- Hip-shot details give way to real physics; the galaxy and setting can shift to fit play.

## Writing style for anything in this repo

- Two spaces after every period.
- No em dashes.
- No contrastive negation ("not X, but Y" or "it's not X, it's Y").
- Version number at the top of every deliverable, bumped on every change.

## Content and license rules

- Lancer Third Party License: no Massif Press art or text (paraphrased concepts only), no Lancer or Massif logos, no claim of affiliation, required credits in the README and in the game's credits.
- The repo is public.  No private information about Nathan or anyone else.
- Hard stops in the game design are recorded in `docs/design/concept.md` (Heroism, Content rating, Ages and lifespans).  Follow them.

## Git

- Commit straight to `main`.  No branches or pull requests unless Nathan asks.
- Small commits with clear messages.
